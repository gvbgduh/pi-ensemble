## Clarification

- [Argon's report](../reports/work-setup-insights.md) is one model's output from the broad analysis brief, not several independent analyses. Orchestrator/Astra intake reviews check the resulting artifact; they do not independently replicate the private raw-corpus analysis.
- The operator personally and carefully observed earlier single Opus 5 use at approximately **$120–130/day**.
- Later setup: approximately **$20–30/day for the Opus 5.5 worker**, **$10–15/day for Argon** (different billing basis), **flat-rate Kimi**, and **$7–12/day for Fable**.
- The operator reports **lower mental load** and **roughly twice-faster results** with the later setup.

## Interpretation and product impact

This is valuable firsthand evidence for persistent specialist collaboration and context distribution, alongside the report's concrete cases. Retrospective telemetry gaps and observational confounds do not disprove or dismiss operator experience. They limit claims about exact comparable billing totals, which model/role change caused savings, or general performance under controlled matched tasks.

Keep the cost ranges and billing-basis qualification rather than fabricating a summed like-for-like API bill or precise percentage saving. No claim the operator's mental load can be reconstructed from transcript counts. Preserve the configurable roster and compare a bounded future workload without demanding that retrospective experience be ignored until a randomized trial exists.
